### Stay Current with the UX Industry

UX design is constantly evolving, so staying updated with new tools, standards, best practices, and industry trends is important for your growth.

#### Thought Leaders in UX Design

Explore organizations, blogs, and resources created by UX professionals to learn best practices, industry insights, and design trends.

* [Nielsen Norman Group](https://www.nngroup.com/) — UX research, usability, articles, reports, and training.
* [UX Collective](https://uxdesign.cc/) — Curated UX articles and insights from designers worldwide.
* [Interaction Design Foundation](https://www.interaction-design.org/) — Evidence-based UX articles, textbooks, and courses.
* [UX Planet](https://uxplanet.org/) — UX fundamentals, career guidance, and advanced design topics.
* [Growth.Design](https://growth.design/) — UX case studies and psychology explained through interactive stories.
* [Case Study Club](https://www.casestudy.club/) — UX and product design case studies for learning and inspiration.
* [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/) — Inspiration from recognized digital and web designs.
* [UX Podcast](https://uxpodcast.com/about/) — Discussions about UX, technology, business, and society.

### Communities of UX Designers

Join UX communities to connect with designers, attend events, get feedback, and expand your professional network.

* [CreativeMornings](https://creativemornings.com/) — Creative meetups, talks, and events around the world.
* [AIGA](https://www.aiga.org/) — Professional design organization offering resources and career development.
* [ADPList](https://adplist.org/) — Mentorship platform connecting designers with experienced professionals.
* [API Who Design](https://www.apiwho.design/) — Community supporting Asian and Pacific Islander designers.
* [Blacks Who Design](https://blackswho.design/) — Community highlighting and supporting Black designers.
* [Ladies That UX](https://ladiesthatux.com/) — Community and events supporting women in UX.
* [Latinxs Who Design](https://www.latinxswhodesign.com/) — Community representing Latinx designers.
* [Techqueria](https://techqueria.org/) — Community supporting Latinx professionals in technology.
* [Women Who Design](https://womenwho.design/) — Directory and community for women in design.
* [Queer Design Club](https://www.queer.design/) — Community supporting LGBTQ+ designers.

# UX Case Study Resources

**UX case-study flow:**

Business problem → Assumptions → User research → Findings → Design decisions → Outcome

## Resources

1. [Google Design — User Research](https://design.google/tags/user-research/)
   - Best for learning user research and challenging assumptions.
2. [Google Design — Field Research](https://design.google/library/beyond-the-desktop-field-research/)
   - Learn how researchers understand users in their real-world context.
3. [UX Design Institute — UX Research Case Studies](https://www.uxdesigninstitute.com/blog/real-world-ux-research-case-studies/)
   - Real-world research examples from companies like Airbnb, Google, and Spotify.
4. [AnswerLab — UX Research Case Studies](https://www.answerlab.com/case-studies)
   - Industry-level UX research examples.
5. [Pratt DX — UX Case Studies](https://prattdx.org/case-studies/)
   - UX design, research, usability testing, accessibility, and more.
6. [UXcel — UX Case Study Examples](https://uxcel.com/blog/ux-case-studies-examples)
   - Learn how UX case studies are structured.
